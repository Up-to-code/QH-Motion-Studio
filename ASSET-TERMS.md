# Character artwork

Fox, Nia and Orbit raster artwork was generated for Ahmed Mansour's DodoMath/QH project from its accepted character references. It is included for operating and evaluating this studio. No separate public redistribution or resale license for these character assets is granted by the code's MIT license. The project owner can choose and document commercial asset terms for a future sale.

Original references, generation prompts, source sheets and technical processing are recorded under animations and docs. No Duolingo mascot or original Duolingo sound recording is included in this studio.
